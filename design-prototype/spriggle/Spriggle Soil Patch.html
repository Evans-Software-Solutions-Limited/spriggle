The soil patch

The wooden frame is gone — the board is now a patch of tilled earth with a grassy rim and tufts. Empty cells read as freshly-dug holes. Here it is at three fill states (same component, same grass treatment).

Empty patch
Fresh-dug soil, no plants. The dug holes + grass tufts carry the look on their own.
Mid-game
Some cells cleared (holes), the rest planted. How the board breathes during play.
Full patch
A freshly refilled 8×8, ready for the next dig.
Reads at a glance: grass rim + tufts frame the play area without a hard border; tilled furrows give the soil direction; each cell is a dug hole so empties never look like “missing” tiles — they look intentional. Plant gems sit in the holes with their own drop shadow, like produce in earth.
